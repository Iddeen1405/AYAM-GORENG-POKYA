About Us

Ayam Goreng Pokya is a family-run fried chicken brand born in Kota Bharu, Kelantan. What started as a humble stall recipe has become a local favourite — crispy skin, juicy meat, and a marinade perfected over years.

We fry fresh all day, every day. No shortcuts, no compromises.
